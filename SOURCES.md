## Credit Risk Data Processing

- Original source: Lending Club historical loan data.
- Kaggle publisher: Wordsforthewise.
- Processed dataset: `data/processed/credit_risk_clean.csv`.
- Scorecard metrics file: `outputs/scorecard/model_metrics.json`.
- Target variable in the scorecard analysis: `not.fully.paid`.
- Variables excluded from the scorecard for leakage concerns: `int.rate` and `credit.policy`.

**Important:** The original source dataset, processed dataset and modelling sample are distinct. The 9,578-row modelling population should not be described as the size of the original Kaggle dataset.
